Features

Everything in one menu.

Aim, visuals, movement and skins, legit or loud, in a menu that reads like a tool. Offsets are resolved at runtime from the game's own schema, so a CS2 update does not take it down.

  • Aim

    Aimbot with field of view, smoothing and curve. Hitbox selection with multipoint. Visible only, not through smoke, hold off while flashed. Humanise with reaction and switch delay.

  • Trigger and autofire

    Triggerbot on the crosshair, silent autofire, no recoil, and auto stop that counter strafes before the shot.

  • Visuals

    Boxes, health and armour bars, names, weapons, distance, skeleton, glow, radar, hit feedback and world colours. Flashed, scoped and defusing called out.

  • Chams

    Painted through the game's own material system. Flat, glow, chrome, wireframe and see through, with separate colours for visible and behind cover, for players, hands and weapons.

  • Movement

    Sub tick bunny hop with hold, toggle and always. Auto strafe that follows the mouse. Quick stop. Third person and a custom field of view.

  • Skins

    Skin changer for weapons, knives and gloves, applied in the game's own renderer.
